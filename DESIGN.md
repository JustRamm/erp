## Brand & Style
This design system defines an industrial yet pristine visual language tailored for next-generation circular manufacturing, resource tracking, and high-precision ERP/MES operations. Rooted in the transformation of discarded ocean and post-consumer plastics into architectural-grade infrastructure, the aesthetic fuses Scandinavian cleanroom clarity with maritime-inspired technical telemetry.

The overall design movement is **Clean High-Tech Modernism** accented by soft oceanic glassmorphism. It dispels the grit typically associated with recycling plants in favor of clinical, hospital-grade precision and digital accountability. Visual spaces leverage deep breathing room, ultra-fine technical dividers, and subtle cyan-tinted atmospheric light to convey positive ecological impact, operational durability, and enterprise-grade reliability. The tone evokes deep trust for supply chain auditors, plant floor supervisors, and executive stakeholders alike.

## Colors
The color architecture rests on a clinical dual-surface philosophy: bright pure white `#FFFFFF` layered over subtle `#F8FAFC` mist canvases, eliminating eye fatigue during 12-hour plant shifts.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary (`#0091FF`)**: Applied to high-intent actions, active pipeline states, real-time extrusion triggers, and dominant interactive elements. When rendering primary heroes or key production KPI milestones, use a 3-stop linear gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #5AAAD4 0%, #3195C9 52%, #0091FF 100%)`.
- **Secondary (`#98CAE4`)**: Applied to structural interactive accents, active navigation indicators, and borders on soft ice pill containers.
- **Tertiary (`#5AAAD4`)**: Utilized for environmental metric telemetry, secondary chart series, and gradient anchors.
- **Neutral Canvas & Typography (`#010B1C` / `#334155`)**: Deep Charcoal Navy `#010B1C` brings stark, authoritative contrast to high-level KPIs and headers. Slate `#334155` provides soft, readable cadence for data grids, telemetry readouts, and operational forms.
- **Semantic Indicators**:
  - `semantic-recycled-emerald` (`#10B981`): Certified net-zero batch completion, virgin plastic offset quotas, and clean QA passes.
  - `semantic-timber-amber` (`#D97706`): Extruder thermal deviations, maintenance queue, and raw shred moisture warnings.
  - `semantic-process-cyan` (`#0091FF`): Real-time wash, pelletization, and compression cycles in progress.
  - `semantic-alert-crimson` (`#EF4444`): PVC polymer contamination, emergency line stoppages, and critical telemetry breach.
  - `semantic-esg-indigo` (`#4F46E5`): Carbon credits generated, public procurement ledger hashes, and municipal audit locks.

## Typography
The system enforces a dual structural hierarchy paired with a technical monospace track:
1. **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly geometric precision, high legibility, and architectural modernity. Tight tracking (-0.015em to -0.03em) keeps dashboard headers compact and authoritative.
2. **Body (`Inter`)**: Chosen for hyper-neutrality, universal screen readability, and dense tabular layout handling.
3. **Telemetry & Cryptographic Identifiers (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict monospace alignment for production lot numbers, thermal extrusion measurements (`°C`), raw tonnage (`MT`), and circular proof-of-impact supply chain ledger hashes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid anchored by responsive outer safe-zones:
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid, `margin: 2rem` (32px), `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Sidebar navigation docks rigidly at 260px or collapses to an 80px technical icon bar.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column grid with `margin: 1.5rem` (24px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Cards reflow into 2-column or 4-column balanced modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single-stack grid, `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px), `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem` (12px). Data tables collapse horizontally to swipeable inline matrices or stacked cards.

Vertical rhythm relies consistently on multiples of 4px / 8px. Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compact form labels and badge interiors, `space-md` for standard card padding, and `space-xl` to segment production stage workflows.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids heavy opaque drop shadows. Depth is maintained through luminous oceanic diffusion, subtle border refraction, and frosted backdrop filters:

- **Surface Tier 0 (Canvas Base)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` foundation. Flat with no shadow.
- **Surface Tier 1 (Elevated Cards & Modules)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, light-cyan-tinted lift:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 145, 255, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(1, 11, 28, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Frosted Sticky Headers & Navigation)**: Glassmorphic translucent surface:
  `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, `border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Surface Tier 3 (Interactive Modals & Slide-over Drawers)**: High-altitude suspension for real-time adjustments:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(1, 11, 28, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 145, 255, 0.08)`, framed by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

## Shapes
The shape strategy balances robust industrial durability with high-tech software fluidity:
- **Cards & Enclosures**: Implement `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.25rem / 16px-20px) to soften high-density metrics and present modern modular dashboard containers.
- **Interactive Buttons & Inputs**: Standardized on `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) to retain technical discipline and quick visual alignment.
- **Badges, Filter Chips, and Status Indicators**: Enforced strict `rounded-full` (9999px) pills to instantly demarcate metadata and categorical tags from rectangular data inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Background is a continuous gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #3195C9 0%, #0091FF 100%)`, text is pure `#FFFFFF`, border is transparent, border radius is 8px (`rounded-lg`). Hover shifts gradient illumination and applies `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(0, 145, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Button**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#010B1C`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover state triggers background `#F8FAFC` and border `#98CAE4`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Text `#334155`, transparent fill. Hover gives `#EBF5FA` pill with text `#0091FF`.

### Status Chips & Pills
- Built with `rounded-full`, 6px vertical and 12px horizontal padding.
- **Default State / Raw Intake**: Background `#EBF5FA`, border `1px solid #98CAE4`, text `#0091FF` in `mono-telemetry-sm`.
- **Verified Circular / Recycled**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#047857`. Accompanied by a 6px pulsating emerald dot.
- **Thermal / Production Warning**: Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`, text `#B45309`.

### Data Cards & Telemetry Tiles
- Background `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-2xl`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, ambient cyan shadow.
- Header row features uppercase tracking `label-caps` in `#334155`, accompanied by an icon badge.
- Center displays `mono-metric-lg` numeric values with small dimensional unit notation (`kg/hr`, `MT`, `%`).
- Footer holds micro sparklines or localized progress trackers toward municipal batch fulfillment.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Inputs**: Height 42px, `rounded-lg`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, font `Inter` 14px with color `#010B1C`.
- **Focus State**: Ring shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 145, 255, 0.15)` and border transitions instantly to `#0091FF`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom 18px boxes with `rounded-md` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio). Inactive border `#CBD5E1`; checked state fills with `#0091FF` displaying a crisp white micro-tick.

### Product-Specific Manufacturing Elements
- **Batch Processing Stepper**: A sequential timeline mapping plastic sorting -> granulation -> high-density compression -> cooling -> CNC routing. Nodes utilize ice-fill capsules connected by 2px `#E2E8F0` tracks that illuminate to vibrant `#0091FF` upon cycle activation.
- **Tonnage Real-Time Feed**: Dedicated compact component with `JetBrains Mono` readouts, live socket connection indicators, and carbon credit balance gauges directly tied to client procurement ledgers.
